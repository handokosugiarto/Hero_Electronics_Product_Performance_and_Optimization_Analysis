# Hero_Electronics_Product_Performance_and_Optimization_Analysis 

## Company Background

#### Hero Electronics is a global electronics retail chain stores that operates across three continents such as North America, Europe, and Australia within eight different countries such as United States, Canada, United Kingdom, France, Netherlands, Germany, Italy, and Australia. Since the establishment in 2016, the company has operated 67 retail stores across continents and  it is still expanding to different areas across the regions. The company is offering a wide range of electronic products such as audio, camera and camcorder, cellular phone, computers, games, toys, home appliances, TV and video, music, movies, 
and audio books. The management had done very well with the sales ranging from US$ 6,947K to US$ 18,265K (2016-2019) and dropped by 49% from US$ 18,265K to US$ 9,295K  after Covid19 pandemic hit in 2020.
